# Vector Motion Craft

Create lively, expressive animated vector graphics that feel crafted by hand rather than generated by a sterile automated tool.

## Visual Styling Guidelines

- **Organic Linework**: Make linework feel smooth and organic rather than rigid. Add a slight wobble to paths to simulate human hand drafting.
- **Curved Edges**: Ensure joints have curved edges. Avoid straight lines where subtle curves could add warmth and character.
- **Composition**: Strive for a balanced composition where elements feel naturally weighted and grounded.

## Motion Dynamics

- **Cinematic Timing**: Animate objects with cinematic timing and natural pacing.
- **Avoid Mechanical Loops**: Avoid repetitive mechanical transitions that make animations look like generic output.
